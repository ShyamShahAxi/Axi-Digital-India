Result by month

INR GL movements by account. Personnel costs capitalised to Asset WIP are a deduction from expenses. The selected month is shaded.

Capitalisation is booked as a month-end entry that reverses on the 1st and is rebooked at a cumulative figure, so single months can swing (April carries the 1 April reversal of the FY26 capitalisation; see review item 3). Quarter and year-to-date columns are the reliable view. No tax is booked during the year.

Trial balance, GL basis

The FY 2025-26 INR GL closing plus the INR movements for the year. It differs from the audited figures only by the auditors' adjustments that are not in Intacct (see Annual rec). Select an account to see its lines for the period.
